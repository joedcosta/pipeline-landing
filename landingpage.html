Key features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

A focused, opinionated tool built around how hospitality businesses actually operate — not a generic CRM bolted onto a spreadsheet.

📋
Client Reservation Pipeline

A visual, stage-based pipeline from first contact to confirmed booking. Track status, notes, next steps and documents for every enquiry in one place.

💰
Dual-Layer Pricing

Maintain a private broker deal price alongside the client-facing collaboration budget. Your margins stay yours — never exposed, always tracked.

📈
EBIT Tracking

Real-time earnings visibility across your whole pipeline. Understand profitability at a glance without opening a spreadsheet.

🔐
Role-Based Access

Admin and Collab roles built in from the ground up. Admins see everything. Collaborators see only what they need. Clean, simple, no compromises.

📎
File Links & Documents

Attach proposals, contracts, menus and seating plans directly to each deal. Everything linked, nothing lost in email threads.

✅
Task & To-Do Management

Per-deal to-dos keep your team accountable. Nothing falls through the cracks when every action has an owner and a deadline.

📱
Mobile-First UI

Designed for operators on the move. The full experience — pipeline, pricing, tasks, documents — works beautifully on any device, anywhere.

☁️
Zero-Dependency Architecture

A single HTML file backed by a Cloudflare Worker. Fast, resilient, no complex infrastructure to manage or maintain. It just works.
